Case study: one dashboard for three shops

Jokima Agrovet runs three branches, each keeping its own paper and spreadsheet records. Here's the problem, what was built, and a live version of the result below.

The problem

Sales and purchases lived in separate books per branch. The owner had no single view of how the business was doing, comparing branches meant manually collecting three sets of numbers.

The solution

Built a centralised dashboard that consolidates sales, purchases, and stock across all three branches into one live reporting layer, with Farmer's Choice products tracked separately as a reseller line.

The result

  • One screen instead of three ledgers
  • Branch comparison in seconds, not days
  • Low-stock issues caught before they cost a sale
